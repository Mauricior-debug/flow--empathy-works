Leadership challenges don’t arrive conveniently between scheduled workshops.

A difficult people issue can emerge on a Monday morning. A senior leader may need confidential support before an important decision. Organisational change can create unexpected pressure. A team may need help working through something difficult. Or a Chief Executive may simply need an experienced, independent person outside the organisation with whom they can think.

That is why we created EmpathyWorks Leadership Subscription Services.

Rather than engaging leadership support only when something has gone wrong, our subscription model gives organisations ongoing access to EmpathyWorks throughout the year.

We get to know your organisation: your people, your culture, your ambitions and the challenges you are facing. So when support is needed, we are not starting from the beginning every time.

Depending on the partnership, your organisation can draw upon leadership coaching, Leadership Diagnostic Assessment, workplace diagnostics, senior-team development, facilitated conversations, organisational consultancy, workshops and practical leadership resources.

But the real value isn’t simply the individual services.

It is the relationship.

Over time, EmpathyWorks becomes a trusted, independent leadership partner, close enough to understand your organisation but sufficiently independent to challenge it.

We can support leaders when things are difficult, help identify emerging issues before they become significant problems, develop leadership capability across the organisation and provide continuity as your organisation grows and changes.

For senior leaders, it also means knowing there is somewhere to go when they need to think: someone who understands the context, someone who will listen, and someone who will challenge when challenge is needed.

From intervention to partnership

Too much leadership and organisational development is reactive.

A problem emerges. A consultant is commissioned. A workshop is delivered. A report is written. And eventually, everyone returns to the pressures of everyday organisational life.

We believe there is another way.

An ongoing leadership partnership creates the opportunity to develop people, leadership and culture over time rather than relying on isolated interventions.

It allows us to notice patterns, revisit commitments, support leaders through changing circumstances and maintain momentum when operational pressures might otherwise push leadership development down the agenda.

It also enables organisations to invest consistently in their people.

Because leadership development shouldn’t only happen when somebody is struggling.

When leaders are nurtured, challenged and given opportunities to develop, they are better equipped to create those same conditions for the people they lead.

And when people experience good leadership, clear expectations, meaningful development, trust and appropriate accountability, organisations create stronger conditions for sustainable performance.

Our Leadership Subscription Services are designed to make ongoing leadership support part of how your organisation operates, rather than something you occasionally buy.

What does a Leadership Partnership give you?

A Leadership Partnership gives your organisation something that traditional consultancy often cannot: continuity.

Instead of beginning again every time support is required, EmpathyWorks develops an understanding of your organisation, your leadership, your people and the environment in which you operate.

That creates a relationship in which support can become more responsive, more relevant and increasingly informed by what we learn together.

Depending on the level of partnership, your organisation can draw upon an agreed combination of the following support.

Leadership Coaching

Confidential one-to-one coaching for senior leaders, managers and emerging leaders.

This creates dedicated space for people to think, reflect, work through difficult situations and continue developing their leadership rather than waiting until a problem becomes a crisis.

Leadership Diagnostic Assessment

Our Leadership Diagnostic Assessment helps individuals understand their leadership more deeply, including behaviours, strengths, development areas and the impact they may have on others.

It can provide a valuable foundation for coaching and wider leadership development.

Workplace Diagnostics and Culture Insight

Understanding how people actually experience your organisation is essential to understanding its culture.

Where appropriate, we can use workplace diagnostics, staff engagement and leadership conversations to identify patterns around culture, trust, relationships, communication, leadership and the overall experience of people at work.

This helps organisations move beyond assumptions and make decisions based on a clearer understanding of what is really happening.

Senior Leadership Team Development

Strong organisations need senior teams capable of leading collectively, not simply a collection of individuals responsible for separate areas.

We work with senior leadership teams to strengthen relationships, improve leadership conversations, explore team dynamics, develop collective responsibility and understand the impact their leadership has across the wider organisation.

Organisational Consultancy and Independent Challenge

Sometimes leaders need an external perspective.

Leadership Partners can draw upon EmpathyWorks for agreed advice, challenge and consultancy around emerging organisational issues, leadership decisions, culture, change, strategy, performance and people.

Because we already understand your organisation, those conversations can begin from a much more informed position.

Leadership Workshops and Development

We provide workshops, facilitated conversations and leadership-development sessions shaped around the needs emerging within your organisation.

Rather than delivering a standard catalogue of training, we respond to what your people and leaders actually need.

Responsive Leadership Support

Not every leadership challenge warrants a consultancy project.

Sometimes a Chief Executive, Director or senior leader simply needs an experienced person outside the organisation to talk something through with: a difficult conversation, a challenging relationship, an important decision, an emerging people issue or a situation where an independent perspective would help.

Our subscription model creates access to that kind of support within the agreed partnership, as part of an established relationship.

Leadership Resources and Practical Tools

Partners can also access practical EmpathyWorks leadership resources, reflective tools and development materials for use with individuals and teams.

These resources help keep leadership development alive between formal coaching, workshops and consultancy interventions.

The value is in the relationship

Any of these services can be commissioned individually.

What makes a Leadership Partnership different is what happens when they are connected over time.

A coaching conversation can inform wider leadership development. A workplace diagnostic can identify themes that shape future work. Senior-team conversations can reveal organisational patterns that require attention. Continued contact allows us to revisit commitments and understand whether agreed changes are actually being experienced by people.

Over time, we develop organisational memory.

We understand where you started, what you have been trying to achieve, what has changed, what has worked and where challenges remain.

That means we can ask better questions, provide more informed challenge, recognise emerging patterns and help maintain momentum.

Most importantly, your leaders know that support is there when they need it, from people who already understand the context.

Not another supplier. Not an occasional intervention. A trusted leadership partner alongside your organisation.

Leadership Partnership Options

Some organisations want access to trusted leadership expertise when challenges arise. Others want a more active programme of leadership development across the year. And some want EmpathyWorks alongside their senior leaders as an ongoing strategic leadership partner.

Built around your organisation

These partnerships are not unlimited consultancy retainers, and they are not rigid packages of hours.

At the beginning of the relationship, we agree the appropriate level of support, the priorities for the partnership and how access to EmpathyWorks will work in practice.

Those priorities can evolve as your organisation evolves.

A partnership might begin with executive coaching and leadership development, move into work around organisational culture, and later require support around change, strategy or a particular leadership challenge.

That flexibility is deliberate.

The partnership gives us enough structure to maintain focus and accountability, while allowing us to respond to the realities of organisational life.

For our more substantial partnerships, we recommend working together over a meaningful period of time. Leadership capability, organisational culture and sustainable change are rarely transformed through short-term interventions.

Within a partnership, support can also include action learning sets, emerging-leader development, leadership self-assessment, team profiling, away days, and short-term advisory work when a deeper piece of help is needed. The level is agreed with you. It is not an unlimited retainer.
