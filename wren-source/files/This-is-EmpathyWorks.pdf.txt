Who we are
EmpathyWorks Leadership Consultancy helps leaders
navigate difficult decisions, build trust and combine

from the University of Cambridge, our work draws on
decades of leadership in demanding environments.

The Human Workspace is our framework for building healthier workplace

thrive together.
