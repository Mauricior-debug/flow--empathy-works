EmpathyWorks 
Leadership Consultancy
At EmpathyWorks, we believe leadership is about 
more than titles and strategies, it is about people, 
culture, and care. Based at Parc Dewi Sant, 
Carmarthen, we are creating the West Wales 
Leadership Excellence Hub, a dedicated space for 
training, consultancy, and collaboration. Our vision is 
to help organisations build safer cultures, stronger 
teams, and workplaces where people thrive. With 
deep leadership experience in some of the UK’s most 
challenging environments, we bring proven tools and 
compassionate approaches to every programme. 
From leadership workshops and team development to 
culture diagnostics and reintegration support, our 
services are designed to: 
•
Strengthen leadership capability at every level 
•
Improve workplace culture and resilience 
•
Support staff wellbeing and prevent burnout 
•
Build confidence, trust, and sustainability 
We work with local authorities, health boards, police, 
probation, charities, and businesses across the 
region, tailoring every programme to real-world 
needs. EmpathyWorks is more than a consultancy, it 
is a movement to change how leadership is lived and 
felt. Our Human Workspace principles, psychological 
safety, clear boundaries, belonging, purpose, and 
care, are at the heart of everything we do.Let’s work 
together to create a future of leadership that is 
compassionate, resilient, and human in our region.  
Our space in Parc Dewi Sant is an inviting 
environment, we offer in person workshops, online 
training, free leadership drop in clinics and support for 
charities and volunteers, along with veterans who are 
looking to find their next post.

Your Team

OUR BESPOKE SERVICES 
Leadership Workshops 
Practical, interactive sessions on compassionate 
leadership, culture change, and resilience. 
The Human Workspace Diagnostic 
A unique culture audit that identifies strengths and 
challenges in your workplace, including understanding 
your culture and giving you a roadmap for safer, stronger 
teams. 
Team Development & Training 
Tailored programmes to build trust, confidence, and 
collaboration across your organisation. 
Boundaries burnout & brave conversations 
Specialist training to protect wellbeing, set healthy limits, 
and equip leaders for difficult conversations. 
Reintegration Support 
Partnership work with probation, police, and communities 
to support safer futures for individuals leaving custody, 
and those on the cusp of the CJS 
Veterans Leadership Programmes 
Dedicated training pathways for veterans and frontline 
staff, turning lived experience into leadership strengths. 
One-to-One Coaching & Mentoring 
Personalised support for leaders at all levels, building 
confidence, clarity, and presence. 
We believe learning should feel welcoming and 
supportive. Our workshops are designed as open, 
safe spaces where people feel looked after as well as 
challenged to grow. We create a positive experience 
with refreshments provided, and for some 
programmes, a shared buffet meal. It’s about care, 
connection, and making leadership development 
something people genuinely enjoy being part of. 
We look forward to supporting organisations across 
West Wales to lead differently. Our ambition is to 
spark a movement for compassionate leadership that 
builds safer cultures, stronger communities, and 
workplaces where people thrive.
