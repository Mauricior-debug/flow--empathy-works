CORE WEBSITE + CRM SERVICES AGREEMENT
EmpathyWorks – Core Live Version

professional before signature.
1. Parties
This Agreement is between:
Client: EmpathyWorks / Steve Robertson (legal entity and registered address to be confirmed).
Service Provider: [Your legal name / company name] (registered address to be confirmed).
Together, the “Parties”.
2. Purpose
The Service Provider will design, build, configure and launch a Core website and connected CRM system for
EmpathyWorks. The purpose is to create a professional commercial website connected to lead capture,
booking, pipeline management and follow-up processes.
The project is based on EmpathyWorks’ existing brand work and September 2026 master copy, so the build is
focused on shaping and implementing the existing material rather than creating the brand or business
proposition from scratch.
3. Project Scope – £2,200 one-time build
The one-time build fee is £2,200. Payment may be made 50% (£1,100) to start and 50% (£1,100) when the
Core system goes live.
Website: A responsive website built around the agreed EmpathyWorks master copy and supplied imagery,
including the core areas: Home; About; One-to-One Leadership Coaching; Organisational Leadership &
Consultancy; Leadership Subscription Services; Speaking, Workshops & Events; and Contact / Book.
CRM and booking: CRM setup, lead capture, a simple sales pipeline, booking flow, contact records, relevant
lead categorisation, booking confirmations and follow-up reminders.
Pipeline: New Lead → Conversation → Opportunity → Client, with interests capable of being tagged to the
agreed service areas.
Tracking and SEO: basic website analytics/tracking and SEO foundations as part of the Core build.
Launch: final review, agreed tweaks within the Core scope, and launch once required client materials and
access have been provided.
The Core build is a defined foundation. It does not include unlimited pages, custom software development,
complex integrations, major new functionality, paid advertising, ongoing content production, or other work
outside the agreed scope unless separately agreed.
4. Timeline
The target delivery period is approximately 2 weeks from kick-off, provided the required copy, imagery,
access and decisions are supplied promptly.
Indicative delivery: Days 1–3 structure and CRM/pipeline/booking setup; Days 4–8 website build and forms;
Days 9–12 automations, tracking, SEO foundations and client review; Days 13–14 final agreed tweaks and
launch.
Delays caused by missing materials, delayed approvals, third-party services or changes outside the agreed
scope may move the launch date.
5. Ongoing Management – £149 per month
The ongoing management fee is £149 per month.
This covers hosting/operation of the website, ongoing management of the website and CRM, booking/pipeline
management, routine maintenance and technical updates, light website/CRM updates, monitoring, and a
simple monthly performance report covering both the website and CRM.

Light updates means reasonable changes such as text, images, links, contact details, small content
adjustments and minor configuration changes that do not require a substantial redesign or new functionality.
Substantial new features, major redesigns, new systems/integrations, significant new page structures or
development work outside the Core system will be discussed and priced separately before work is
undertaken.
No additional work outside the agreed scope will be charged without the Client being informed and agreeing
to it first.
6. AI – Optional
AI support is not required for the Core service.
If the Client chooses to activate optional AI support, the expected additional cost is approximately £20–£40
per month depending on usage. The Client remains in control of whether this is activated.
7. Ownership and Access
The Client owns the website and the business/customer data belonging to EmpathyWorks. The Service
Provider is providing development and management services and does not acquire ownership of the Client’s
business assets.
The Client will have appropriate access to the website and CRM/backend. A shared development/ownership
email may be created so that key assets and accounts remain accessible to the Client.
The Service Provider will not intentionally lock the Client out of its website, CRM or business/customer data.
On termination, the Service Provider will cooperate reasonably with the Client to provide access and/or export
relevant website and CRM data and assets that are within the Client’s ownership and control, subject to any
third-party platform limitations and any outstanding amounts due.
8. Domain, Third-Party Services and Accounts
Where possible, domains and key service accounts should be registered using Client-controlled details or the
agreed shared ownership email.
Third-party services may have their own terms, pricing, technical limitations and data-processing
arrangements. Any material third-party cost outside the agreed £149 monthly service will be discussed with
the Client before it is incurred.
The Service Provider cannot guarantee the continued availability or pricing of third-party platforms.
9. Data Protection, GDPR and Security
The Parties acknowledge that the CRM may contain names, email addresses, telephone numbers and
potentially information relating to coaching clients or enquiries.
The Parties will use the system in accordance with applicable UK data-protection requirements. The Client
remains responsible for determining the lawful purpose and lawful basis for collecting and using its customer
and coaching information, providing appropriate privacy information to individuals, and ensuring that
information supplied to the system is appropriate for the service.
The Service Provider will apply reasonable technical and organisational measures appropriate to the services
it provides and will only access Client data as reasonably necessary to provide, maintain or support the
website and CRM.
The Parties will cooperate reasonably regarding data-security incidents, data requests and data
retention/deletion. Where the nature of the processing requires a separate data-processing agreement, the
Parties may enter into one.
10. Client Responsibilities
The Client will provide accurate copy, imagery, branding materials, access credentials and approvals needed
to complete the project.
The Client is responsible for the accuracy and legality of its business content, claims, coaching information,
testimonials, privacy notices, terms and other materials it supplies.
The Client should not place highly sensitive or unnecessary personal information into the CRM where it is not
required for the agreed workflow.

11. Review, Changes and Approval
The Client will have an opportunity to review the website and CRM during the build and before launch.
Reasonable final tweaks that fall within the Core scope are included. Requests that materially expand the
scope will be discussed before implementation.
Once the Client approves the Core system for launch, the remaining build balance becomes payable and the
system can be moved to live operation.
12. Termination and Exit
The Client may end the ongoing management service by giving 30 days’ written notice, unless the Parties
agree otherwise in writing.
Either Party may terminate for a material breach that is not remedied within a reasonable period after written
notice.
Termination does not transfer ownership of the Client’s website, domain or customer/business data away
from the Client.
Any unpaid amounts for work already completed or services already provided remain payable.
13. Fees and Payment
Core build: £2,200 one-time.
Suggested payment schedule: £1,100 at project start and £1,100 at go-live.
Ongoing management: £149 per month, beginning when the website/CRM goes live unless otherwise agreed
in writing.
Optional AI: approximately £20–£40 per month only if activated by the Client.
Any agreed additional work or third-party costs will be confirmed before being incurred.
14. No Lock-In
The commercial intention of this arrangement is that the Client receives a long-term foundation it owns while
the Service Provider provides ongoing management and support. The Client is not required to remain with the
Service Provider indefinitely in order to retain ownership of its website or business/customer data.
15. Liability and Service Limitations
The Service Provider will provide the services with reasonable care and skill. The Service Provider is not
responsible for outages, failures, pricing changes or limitations caused by third-party hosting, CRM, booking,
email, domain, analytics or other external services.
Nothing in this Agreement excludes or limits liability where doing so would be unlawful.
