5. Essential Automations & Follow-Up 
The CRM is designed to remove the need to remember who needs attention and when. 
Example: New enquiry → CRM creates the lead → lead is categorised → conversation is booked → 
confirmation/reminder is sent → next action is recorded → follow-up reminder appears when it is due. 
The CRM should answer: “Who do I need to follow up with today?”

SEO is considered from the beginning. The website will be structured so search engines can understand

9. Ownership, Export & Future-Proofing 
You are not locked into us. 
The EmpathyWorks website and business/customer data remain yours. 

If you ever decide to stop using our managed service, you can export the website and host it anywhere you 
choose. If you prefer, we can also continue to host it elsewhere for you. 
CRM data can be exported in a usable format whenever you need it. The CRM also has daily backups, 
providing additional protection for your data. 
In short, you can move the website and take your CRM data with you rather than being locked into one 
platform or provider.
