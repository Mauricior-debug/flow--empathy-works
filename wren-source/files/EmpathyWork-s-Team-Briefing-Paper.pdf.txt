Strengthening People, Culture and Community through Leadership
Who we are
EmpathyWorks Leadership Consultancy is a specialist leadership and organisational
development consultancy. We work with leaders, teams and organisations to strengthen
leadership capability, improve workplace culture and create healthier, more sustainable
environments in which people can thrive.
Founded by Steve Robertson, a former Prison Governor, national operational leader and
graduate of the University of Cambridge where he studied Humanitarian Leadership.
EmpathyWorks was established from a simple observation. Across every organisation Steve
led, whether within prisons, criminal justice partnerships, national operational services or
community settings, the greatest challenges were rarely technical. More often, they were
human. Leadership, culture, communication, trust and relationships frequently determined
whether organisations succeeded or struggled. Supported by a fantastic team consisting of
Neil, Kellie and Steve, all fantastic leaders within their own backgrounds. The team brings a
richly diverse approach to supporting organisations. 
Today, EmpathyWorks supports organisations across health and social care, local
government, criminal justice, housing, education, charities, social enterprises and
community services. Although structured as a Community Interest Company, we are first
and foremost a leadership consultancy, helping organisations develop their people,
strengthen culture and build leadership confidence at every level.
EmpathyWorks is supported by a growing team of coaches, mentors, facilitators
and organisational development practitioners who share a commitment to
strengthening people, culture and community through leadership.

Our Approach
At EmpathyWorks, we believe that leadership development cannot be separated from
organisational culture, and that organisational culture cannot be separated from human
experience. Too often, organisations attempt to solve complex challenges through
processes, structures and strategies alone, whilst overlooking the human factors that
ultimately determine whether change succeeds or fails. Our work focuses on helping
organisations understand and strengthen the relationships, behaviours and cultures that
sit beneath day-to-day performance.
Our approach has been shaped by decades of leadership experience in some of the most
challenging environments in public service. Steve and Neil’s career’s included leading
prisons, managing major operational challenges and supporting organisations where staff
were routinely exposed to trauma, loss, violence, uncertainty and significant organisational
pressure. These experiences reinforced a simple but powerful lesson: when people feel
psychologically safe, valued, connected and supported, they perform better, communicate
more effectively and are better able to navigate challenge and change. Kellie has been
instrumental in helping individuals through personal trauma, she is also our mentoring
and ‘Reflective Spaces’ lead. Steve is an award winning teacher, who has challenged the
norms of teaching and created significant amounts of innovative thought to how we teach
our younger generations. 
Alongside practical leadership experience, our work is informed by research and learning
drawn from humanitarian leadership, organisational development, psychology and
workplace culture. We are particularly interested in the relationship between leadership
behaviour, employee wellbeing, organisational performance and community outcomes.
Rather than offering off-the-shelf solutions, we work alongside organisations to understand
their unique context and develop practical, sustainable approaches that strengthen both
people and performance.
Ultimately, our aim is not simply to develop better leaders. It is to help create healthier
organisations, stronger cultures and more connected communities through leadership
that is thoughtful, compassionate and effective.
The Human Workspace
The Human Workspace is the leadership and culture framework that sits at the heart of
much of our work. It was developed through Steve's experience of leading within some of
the most demanding environments in public service, particularly prisons, where leadership
was tested daily against the realities of trauma, violence, deaths in custody, self-harm,
burnout and significant organisational pressure.
Over many years, it became increasingly clear that whilst policies, procedures and
performance measures were important, workplace culture often determined how
effectively organisations responded to challenge. Teams that felt trusted, supported and
psychologically safe consistently performed better than those operating within cultures
characterised by fear, blame or disconnection.
The Human Workspace helps organisations explore and strengthen the conditions that
allow people and performance to thrive together. It focuses on themes such as trust,
belonging, wellbeing, accountability, visibility, communication and reflection, recognising
that healthy cultures do not happen by accident. They are created intentionally through
leadership behaviours, organisational practice and everyday human interactions.

What We Do
EmpathyWorks supports organisations to strengthen leadership, improve workplace culture
and create environments where people can perform well without compromising their
wellbeing. Our work is built on the belief that leadership development should not be
separated from organisational culture, and that sustainable performance is achieved when
people feel valued, connected and supported.
We work with leaders at every level, from emerging managers to chief executives, providing
executive coaching, leadership mentoring and bespoke leadership development
programmes. Alongside individual development, we support leadership teams to improve
communication, strengthen accountability, navigate organisational change and build
cultures characterised by trust, psychological safety and shared purpose.
Our organisational development work helps organisations better understand the reality of
their workplace culture. Through leadership assessments, culture diagnostics, employee
surveys and facilitated conversations, we provide insight into the experiences of staff and the
factors influencing organisational performance. This work enables leaders to identify
strengths, understand challenges and develop practical, evidence-informed plans for
improvement.
As EmpathyWorks has grown, a number of specialist services have emerged. Our Leadership
Subscription Service provides organisations with ongoing access to leadership expertise,
coaching, facilitation and organisational support, creating long-term partnerships rather
than one-off consultancy interventions. Reflective Spaces offers structured opportunities for
individuals and teams to step away from operational pressures, think clearly, process
challenge and strengthen resilience. We also deliver action learning sets, leadership clinics,
team development workshops, organisational away days and bespoke consultancy support
tailored to the needs of individual organisations.
Alongside our consultancy work, the EmpathyWorks Leadership Institute provides a physical
space for leadership development, coaching, mentoring, workshops, collaborative learning
and cross-sector conversation. Together, these services allow us to support individuals,
teams and organisations at every stage of their leadership and organisational development
journey.
Looking Forward
EmpathyWorks was founded on a belief that leadership matters. When leadership improves,
organisations become healthier, cultures become stronger and communities benefit.
Everything we do is built around helping organisations realise that potential.
As we continue to grow, our ambition is straightforward. We want to support leaders who
are thoughtful, compassionate and effective. We want to help organisations create cultures
where people can flourish. And we want to contribute to a wider movement that places
humanity, wellbeing and connection at the heart of leadership. Through our consultancy
work, our Leadership Institute and our growing network of partnerships, we are committed
to strengthening people, culture and community through leadership.
Our ambition is to establish EmpathyWorks and the Leadership Institute as a recognised
centre for leadership excellence, organisational development and human-centred
leadership practice in Wales.
