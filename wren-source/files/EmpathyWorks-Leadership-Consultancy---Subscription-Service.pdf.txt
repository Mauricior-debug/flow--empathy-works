Our Leadership subscription levels
EmpathyWorks offers three tiers of leadership (below)
partnerships, designed to reflect the different needs,
pressures, and ambitions of organisations at different
stages of growth.
Each level provides structured leadership support,
reflective space, and access to experienced guidance,
with increasing depth of support as organisational
responsibility and complexity grow.
All subscription levels are grounded in the same
purpose: to strengthen leadership, sustain people, and
support healthy organisational culture over time.
Leadership Community
Shared learning, reflection, and connection
A structured leadership community providing facilitated reflective
space, peer learning, and practical development.
Ideal  for  organisations  seeking  consistent  support,  fresh
perspective,  and  connection  with  other  leaders  facing  similar
challenges.
Leadership Development Partner
Dedicated support for strengthening leadership capability
Builds  on  the  Leadership  Community  by  adding  personalised
leadership support and development.
Provides  structured  one-to-one  leadership  conversations,
development insight, and practical guidance to help leaders grow
in confidence, clarity, and effectiveness.
Organisational Partner
Embedded leadership support for complex organisations
A  deeper  partnership  focused  on  the  leadership  health  of  the
organisation as a whole.
Includes support for senior leaders and leadership teams, helping
align culture, strengthen decision-making, and navigate growth,
change, and organisational complexity.
