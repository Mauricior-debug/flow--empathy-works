About EmpathyWorks

We believe compassion, empathy and humanity are not alternatives to high standards, accountability and performance.

Leadership shaped by the real world

EmpathyWorks Leadership Consultancy was created from a belief that leadership can, and should, be both human and effective.

We have spent much of our professional lives leading people in environments where decisions matter, pressure is real and the consequences of leadership can be significant.

Those experiences have shaped a simple philosophy.

People perform at their best when they experience good leadership.

That means leadership with clarity and purpose. Leadership that creates trust and develops people. Leadership that is prepared to listen and understand, but is equally prepared to challenge, make difficult decisions and hold people accountable.

We don’t believe leaders should have to choose between compassion and performance.

We believe the strongest leadership creates the conditions for both.

Why EmpathyWorks exists

EmpathyWorks was created to take those lessons beyond the environments in which they were learned.

Across organisations of every kind, leaders are dealing with many of the same fundamental challenges: pressure, change, difficult relationships, organisational culture, performance, uncertainty and the responsibility of leading other people.

The context may change, but many of the leadership questions remain remarkably similar.

How do you create a culture where people feel valued while maintaining high expectations?

How do you support people without avoiding accountability?

How do you lead through uncertainty when you don’t have every answer?

How do you recognise when the culture you intended to create is different from the culture your people are actually experiencing?

And how do you look after and develop the people who ultimately determine whether your organisation succeeds?

These are the questions that sit at the heart of EmpathyWorks.

We bring together extensive real-world leadership experience, academic insight, evidence and practical organisational development to help leaders and organisations find answers that work in their own context.

We don’t believe in leadership theatre.

We believe in leadership that people can actually experience.

Steve Robertson — Founder & Director

Steve Robertson is the Founder and Director of EmpathyWorks Leadership Consultancy.

His career has included more than three decades of senior public-service leadership, including leading HMP Guys Marsh and holding a range of senior leadership responsibilities across the UK Prison Service.

During the COVID-19 pandemic, Steve took on national leadership responsibility for the UK’s quarantine hotel service, coordinating complex operational delivery and multiple agencies during an unprecedented national emergency.

Those experiences developed a particular interest in what happens to leadership when organisations and people are placed under pressure: how cultures respond, how leaders behave, and why the human experience of work and organisational performance are so closely connected.

Steve went on to complete a Master of Studies in Humanitarian Leadership at the University of Cambridge, bringing academic study together with decades of practical leadership experience.

Through EmpathyWorks, he now works with individual leaders, senior teams and organisations, combining coaching, leadership development, organisational diagnostics and consultancy with the practical insight that comes from having led when circumstances were far from straightforward.

His philosophy is grounded in responsibility rather than status.

Leadership, for Steve, is about creating the conditions in which other people can perform, grow and succeed, particularly when the stakes are high.

Neil Lavis — Senior Consultant

Neil Lavis brings more than three decades of leadership experience across the criminal justice system in the UK and internationally.

His career has included leading HMP Swansea and HMP Leyhill, as well as five years as Director within the HM Cayman Islands Prison Service.

Across those roles, Neil has led organisations through significant change and transformation while consistently demonstrating that humanity, accountability, safety and performance do not have to compete with one another.

At HMP Leyhill, he led a values-driven transformation that resulted in the prison becoming a high-performing establishment. In the Cayman Islands, he led significant organisational change following a critical inspection, helping to build a healthier and more accountable culture while strengthening rehabilitation and conditions for staff and those in custody.

Neil’s leadership style is calm, thoughtful and deeply people-centred.

He brings particular experience in organisational culture, rehabilitation, staff engagement, leadership through change and creating environments where people are given the opportunity and support to develop.

At EmpathyWorks, Neil brings that experience into our work with leaders and organisations, providing coaching, consultancy, leadership development and independent challenge.

The wider team

EmpathyWorks is more than a team. It is a group of people committed to helping others feel heard, supported, and stronger in difficult moments. We lead with humanity, trust, and a shared belief that compassionate leadership genuinely changes lives.

Supported by Neil, Kellie and Steve, the team brings a richly diverse approach to supporting organisations. Kellie has been instrumental in helping individuals through personal trauma. She is also our mentoring and Reflective Spaces lead.

EmpathyWorks is supported by a growing team of coaches, mentors, facilitators and organisational development practitioners, and by our office manager, who share a commitment to strengthening people, culture and community through leadership.

Founded by Steve Robertson, a former Prison Governor, national operational leader and graduate of the University of Cambridge where he studied Humanitarian Leadership, EmpathyWorks was established from a simple observation. Across the organisations he led, the greatest challenges were rarely technical. More often, they were human.

Although the company is registered both as EmpathyWorks Leadership Consultancy Ltd and with a Community Interest Company number, we are first and foremost a leadership consultancy, helping organisations develop their people, strengthen culture and build leadership confidence at every level.

Different experiences. Shared philosophy.

Steve and Neil have followed different leadership journeys, but the principles connecting their work are remarkably consistent.

  • People
  • Culture
  • Behaviour

We believe people matter.

We believe culture matters.

We believe leadership behaviour matters.

We believe people should be nurtured and developed, but we also believe in accountability, responsibility and high expectations.

And we believe organisational performance and the experience of the people responsible for delivering it are fundamentally connected.

That philosophy sits behind everything EmpathyWorks does.

Whether we are coaching one leader, working alongside an entire organisation, providing an ongoing Leadership Partnership or speaking to a room full of people, our purpose remains the same:

Helping people lead well in hard places.
